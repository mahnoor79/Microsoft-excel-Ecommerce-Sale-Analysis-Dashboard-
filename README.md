# 📊 Microsoft-excel-Ecommerce-Sale-Analysis-Dashboard-

Welcome to the Ecommerce Sales Analysis Dashboard, an Excel-based business intelligence project where raw sales data has been transformed into meaningful insights using visual tools like charts and graphs. This project is ideal for understanding customer behavior, product performance, and regional trends in ecommerce.

## 🎯 Project Title
"Ecommerce Sales Performance Dashboard Using Excel"

## 📌 Objective
1) To analyze ecommerce sales data in order to:

2) Identify top-performing products and regions

3) Track monthly sales trends

4) Understand customer segment behavior

5) Examine order priority impacts on sales

6) This analysis helps make data-driven business decisions.

## 🧩 Problem Statement
Companies often struggle with making sense of large volumes of sales data. Without proper visualization, opportunities and weak areas are hidden. This dashboard was created to solve that by offering an organized view of key performance indicators (KPIs) through interactive charts.

#📈 Key Insights with Numbers
## ✅ 1. Sales by Category
Technology generated the highest sales: $836,154.97

Furniture contributed $741,999.80

Office Supplies brought in $719,047.03

📌 Insight: Technology outperformed all categories, indicating strong demand in electronics.

## ✅ 2. Monthly Sales Trend
November had the highest sales: $120,914.73

December came second: $111,014.41

February had the lowest: $54,199.66

📌 Insight: There’s a noticeable holiday peak in November and December, and a slow dip early in the year.

## ✅ 3. Sales by Region
West region topped with $725,458.95

East followed with $678,781.03

South reported $391,721.91

Central was the lowest with $501,240.91

📌 Insight: The West region is the strongest market, while Central lags behind.

## ✅ 4. Customer Segment-wise Sales
Consumer segment led with $1,151,956.87

Corporate generated $709,370.96

Home Office totaled $435,874.20

📌 Insight: Consumers are the primary buyers; Corporate and Home Office are smaller but notable segments.

## ✅ 5. Order Priority Sales
Medium priority orders: $1,288,130.37

High priority orders: $441,238.50

Critical: $356,041.76

Low: $211,790.40

📌 Insight: Most orders are Medium priority, but High and Critical orders are significant in value.

# 📊 Graphs & Charts with Insights
## 📌 Clustered Column Chart: Sales by Category
Shows that Technology sales are nearly 13% higher than Furniture.

Quick comparison makes it easy to identify leading product lines.

## 📌 Line Chart: Monthly Sales Trend
Sharp sales growth in Q4, peaking in November.

Helps in seasonal marketing and stock management planning.

## 📌 Pie Chart: Sales by Region
West (32%) and East (30%) dominate the sales.

Central region makes up just 22% of total revenue, a clear growth opportunity.

## 📌 Stacked Column Chart: Sales by Order Priority
Most sales occur under Medium priority, accounting for over 50% of total revenue.

Critical orders, although fewer, show higher per-order value.

## 📌 Bar Chart: Customer Segment Sales
Consumer segment contributes ~50% of overall sales.

Provides direction for customer segmentation in marketing strategies.

## 📌 Summary
The Excel dashboard provides a 360-degree view of ecommerce sales activity. It clearly identifies top revenue sources by product, region, time, and customer segment. The charts enhance decision-making by simplifying complex data into easy-to-digest visuals.
